# Third-party data and models

This repository does **not** redistribute the full third-party datasets or pretrained model weights used in the experiments.

- **CoarseWSD-20** is loaded by the notebooks from the Hugging Face dataset identifier `kiamehr74/CoarseWSD-20`.
- **RAW-C** is downloaded by the validation notebook from the public repository maintained by its authors.
- Pretrained encoders are loaded from their public model identifiers: `bert-base-uncased`, `roberta-base`, and `microsoft/deberta-v3-base`.

Users are responsible for checking and complying with the licenses and terms attached to those external resources.
