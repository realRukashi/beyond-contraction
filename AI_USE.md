# Generative-AI assistance disclosure

This independent study used generative-AI systems extensively for research scaffolding, code drafting and debugging, literature-search assistance, analysis planning, and manuscript editing. The initial research question and geometric intuition originated with Hồ Ngọc Huy.

The repository is released to make the resulting experiments inspectable rather than to imply that every methodological choice was independently developed without assistance. Executed notebooks are included as provenance. Readers should treat the manuscript as an exploratory preprint and independently verify claims that matter to downstream work.

Any future venue submission should be updated to satisfy that venue's current authorship and generative-AI disclosure policy.
